# Public site redesign — home (/), engagers (/join), free trial (/try)

Mode: Persuade. Two visitors: (1) Nigerian business owners and creators deciding whether to trust a paid-engagement service after being burned by bot panels; (2) phone-first Nigerians (students, corps members, side-hustlers) deciding whether Primeloop is a real way to earn. Both arrive on mobile data, distrustful. Action: claim the free trial (clients) / create an account (engagers). Proof available: the free trial itself, live activity and payouts from the real database, the comparison against bot panels, the refund promise. Not available: real testimonials, real counts, real payout totals (must never be invented).

The user rejected the previous look (Fraunces serif, cream cards, pill gradient CTA, "AI-generated" feel) and asked for a mature, modern, professional feel with real photography. Direction pinned by the user: **modern fintech** (crisp, confident, mostly white and navy, precise grid). Photography: licensed Unsplash portraits verified as taken in Nigeria, never captioned as customers or engagers. Scope: public pages first; dashboards later.

Unresolved: real photos and testimonials from the user's own engagers/clients replace the stock ones when they exist; fake testimonials removed until then.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Trust through legibility. One confident sans, one navy field, real faces, and proof shown as product UI rather than described. Refuses the category-default AI arrangement: warm cream ground, high-contrast serif display, pill gradient button, and a hero-metric stat trio.

OWN-WORLD: White ground with cool-tint bands; deep navy (#1c2340) is the only large colour field (comparison and closing bands); signal orange (#e0632b) carries exactly one primary action per view, filled, with navy text for contrast. Bricolage Grotesque display (heavy, tight) over Hanken Grotesk text, tabular numerals for money. Controls 10px radius, cards 16px, photos 20px, hairline borders, almost no shadow except the proof card that floats over the photo. Icons are drawn SVG in one stroke weight. No mono, no gradient text.

STORY: The visitor sees a real person and a live verification card, understands that real people do the engagement and each one has a screenshot behind it, believes it because they can try it free, and taps "Try it free" (or "Start earning").

FIRST VIEWPORT: 64px sticky header (wordmark left, three links, Log in, orange "Try it free"). Below, a 12-column split: left seven columns hold the H1 at 56/60px heavy, a 18px lead, the orange CTA with an inline text link for the paid path, and three one-line assurances with drawn checks; right five columns hold a 2:3 portrait photo with a 20px radius and a white proof card overlapping its lower-left edge showing live verification rows (or a clearly labelled example).

FORM: Fintech product page with photography, user-pinned (position 1 of the pinned direction; no dice roll). Seed key: user-pinned.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
